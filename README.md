## What I Do

- **Lead full-stack delivery** of DeFi products from architecture to deployment.
- **Design and ship dApps** in React/TypeScript with multi-chain wallet flows (EVM + Starknet).
- **Build backend services and APIs** in Node.js/Express backed by PostgreSQL, with strong attention to authentication, hardening, and operational reliability.
- **Integrate on-chain protocols and off-chain venues** — DEX aggregators (Kyberswap, AVNU), perpetual exchanges (Bybit, Paradex, Lighter, Extended), cross-chain messaging (LayerZero), and oracle automation (Tenderly Web3 Actions).
- **Lead teams**: architecture decisions, code review standards, mentoring, and delivery planning.

## How I Work

- **Technical leadership** — architecture, code review, and engineering standards across teams.
- **Pragmatic delivery** — favor simple, observable systems over premature abstraction; ship and iterate.
- **Quality bar** — typed code, automated tests, linting, and review gates as defaults, not afterthoughts.
- **Cross-functional collaboration** — work closely with product, research, and operations to turn requirements into shipped features.
